# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Cup, Xfinity, and Trucks schedules, sessions, and selected-series live snapshots.
- Historical pit-road/service timing, tire changes, and race notes.
- Consecutive rolling pace and best N-lap averages, driver pace comparisons,
  pit-interval summaries, and leader-lap flag periods.
- Season result collection and driver-ID aggregates, with explicit unavailable-race
  diagnostics. Earned points are not official playoff standings.
- Position, pace comparison, and rolling-average plotting helpers.
- LiveHistory tracks observed lap times, corrections, and missed laps.
- Recordings can resume with race/run validation and optional deduplication.
- Bristol comparison example and regression tests for analysis and recording.

## 0.1.0

- Cup schedules, race laps, weekend results, flags, cache, and live recording.
- Lap selectors and basic plotting.
- Hocevar Bristol chart example.
